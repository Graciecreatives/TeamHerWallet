## Asset choices

- Café portrait: 640px and 1200px wide, with a lower-half crop to preserve the creator and laptop rather than the empty upper window.
- Home workspace: 480px and 960px wide. The same quiet, naturally lit photograph supports signup and a course concept.
- Pottery: 480px and 960px wide, cropped around the hands and wheel.
- Explicit intrinsic dimensions and reserved aspect ratios prevent image layout shifts. The hero loads eagerly with high fetch priority. Below-the-fold imagery is lazy-loaded and asynchronously decoded.
- Each asset has a 250 KB upper budget enforced by a native test. Color comes from the real photographs against blush/lilac surfaces, not strong color filters applied to people.
